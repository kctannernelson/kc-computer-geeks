About KC Computer Geeks

Local computer service with clear communication.

KC Computer Geeks provides computer repair, upgrades, cleaning, data transfer, and custom PC building for customers in the Kansas City area.

The goal is simple: explain the issue, give you an estimate before work starts, and help you make the repair or upgrade decision that makes sense for your computer.

Meet the Owner

Tanner Nelson

KC Computer Geeks is owned and operated by Tanner Nelson, a cybersecurity student with a strong interest in computers, technology, troubleshooting, and hands-on PC work. Tanner started KC Computer Geeks to turn that experience into a local service focused on helping people get more life, performance, and reliability out of their computers.

From diagnosing everyday PC problems to upgrading hardware and planning custom gaming or workstation builds, Tanner takes a practical approach: understand what the customer actually needs, explain the options clearly, and recommend a solution that makes sense for the system and budget.

That same interest in cybersecurity and IT also shapes the way KC Computer Geeks approaches computer service — with attention to system health, reliability, good setup practices, and clear communication throughout the process.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you charge for diagnostics?

Diagnostics are free when you move forward with the repair. Diagnostic-only service starts at $25.

Do you supply replacement parts?

Parts can be discussed as part of your estimate. Pricing shown on this site is for labor unless otherwise noted.

Can you build a PC if I already have some of the parts?

Yes. Include the parts you already own in your message so compatibility can be checked before the build begins.

How much does a custom PC cost?

The build labor fee is $100 plus the cost of parts. The total depends on your budget and performance goals.
